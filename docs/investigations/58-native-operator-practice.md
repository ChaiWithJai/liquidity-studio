## Current content-work walkthrough

Updated for merged main `0f1c21d` (PR197/198/201). These steps describe the current controls. The founder path was exercised by engineers in the local developer profile, including both brands and real Penpot edits. It is not a completed learner assessment. Nicole’s paired marketer/native access is still pending; do not offer her someone else’s persona or credentials as a substitute.

1. **Choose your work.** Use your authorized workspace and brand, then open the story from the backlog. A Studio link retains its story, format and preview page across reload and browser Back/Forward. In the local developer application, choose the intended local persona if prompted; the link returns to the same task. This local selector is not Google sign-in or a production identity pairing.
2. **Prepare and inspect the copy.** If there is no saved campaign, choose **Prepare source preview** when available, then **Refresh preparation status**. A ready preview is still unreviewed. Read **Evidence and unresolved questions**, open the original source, and check dates, context and missing information. Use **Preview format** and **Page** to inspect all four formats. Edit the wording, choose **Save preview edits**, then reload to check that the saved wording is retained. A source excerpt is not automatically a complete conclusion.
3. **Review deliberately.** Choose **Review saved draft and evidence** only after saving. Inspect **All saved format copy**, select the correct content classification, and confirm only the review you actually performed. **Confirm reviewed copy and continue to handoff** saves that exact editorial copy. It does not approve a layout or publish. Clinical research keeps its separate current-founder clinical confirmation requirement; use a nonclinical example for this introductory lesson.
4. **See the work first.** In the saved campaign, choose the format above the artwork. The **Studio preview** is the saved Studio copy. Open **Preview settings** for phone width, zoom, safe zones, caption ruler and artifact details. The three frequent task actions are **Review sources**, **Edit wording** and **Preview & validation**. **Save artwork changes** appears when artwork is dirty. For reviewed prepared copy, use **Edit reviewed copy** to start the explicit new review path; do not expect direct edits to replace the reviewed generation silently.
5. **Choose the design path.** Where the configured Penpot feature is available, choose **Create design in Penpot**, then **Open design in Penpot**. Reopening returns to the same saved file. Edit a heading in Penpot, wait for **Saved**, reload that document, and check the text before returning to Studio. Penpot edits stay in Penpot: the Studio preview and **Export Studio preview PNG/ZIP/script** still use the saved Studio copy. **Edit layout in Studio** is a separate optional native editor when enabled, not a required step before Penpot. Do not infer that a disabled or absent editor has been provisioned.
6. **Find secondary actions.** Open **More actions** for **Validation results**, **Export handoff**, **Plan in calendar** and **Return to backlog**. In a keyboard lesson, open the disclosure, choose the action, and check that focus reaches the requested section. Completed source review is under **Review details**; **Linked design tools** contains the existing connection, update and history controls. Open these when needed rather than treating every panel as a step to complete.
7. **Leave a recoverable boundary.** Save, reload and reopen the same story/design. Unsaved edits block browser Back/Forward; save or explicitly discard them rather than navigating around the warning. Calendar placement sets a date, an export downloads an artifact, and neither publishes it. Native layout approval and verified-PNG signoff remain their own authorized workflow.

Observed product references: [PR197 create/edit/reopen and screen review](https://github.com/ChaiWithJai/liquidity-studio/pull/197), [PR198 compact task actions](https://github.com/ChaiWithJai/liquidity-studio/pull/198), and [PR201 preview-first layout](https://github.com/ChaiWithJai/liquidity-studio/pull/201). ## Worked example → explain → modify → independent practice

Choose one nonclinical source story and one disposable design. The session's useful output is a source-linked saved preview plus a reopened design or review-required planning brief, not a test score. Use the existing local Founder/Marketer chooser when already configured; do not wait for invitations or ask someone to share an SSO session. Use only that persona's existing authorized workspace. The trainer chooses the story and explains why the task matters before showing the controls.

1. **Watch a worked example.** Open the Beyoncé/AMC source story. Use **Prepare source preview** if needed, inspect the dated October 2, 2023 announcement and unresolved questions, then inspect Feed, Carousel, Story and Reel boards. Keep December 1, 2023 as historical context; do not invent revenue shares or merchandise figures. Save one clear heading edit such as “Film announcement · 2023”, reload, and show the retained wording and source. Explain that ready artwork is still unreviewed. If the job fails, inspect Jobs and the retained failure before using an offered Retry; do not queue duplicate work or reset a quota.
2. **Explain before clicking.** Ask the learner to point to the source, its date, the saved candidate revision, the unreviewed state and an excluded claim. Ask which output changes when a Penpot heading is edited and which output changes when the calendar date moves. Feedback is concrete: the source excerpt supports the dated film announcement; it does not support a private compensation figure. Have the learner correct the explanation before continuing, without a time penalty.
3. **Modify with guidance.** The learner edits a caption while keeping its date/limitations, selects **Save preview edits**, reloads and compares the exact saved text. For a nonclinical draft that the learner actually reviewed, use **Review saved draft and evidence** and the explicit confirmation, then **Create/Open design in Penpot**. Change one heading, wait for Saved, reload the same native file and compare. The Studio preview remains the reviewed Studio copy. Alternatively, in **Edit layout in Studio**, the compact story/format header and **Return to saved copy and preview** surround the canvas; **Review saved layout**, **PNG export**, **Brand styles** and **Upload rights** are supporting disclosures, not steps to complete every time. Save or explicitly discard before returning.
4. **Do an independent exercise.** Give the learner the LuckyChap/Hasbro source story and a different format. Their brief: retain the 2024 announcement context, exclude Margot Robbie compensation/profit claims, make one useful wording change, save/reload and reopen the same chosen design. Provide the desired outcome but no click-by-click prompts. The learner explains their evidence and what remains unapproved. Cover other formats in later short sessions rather than making recovery, sound and source review one long exercise.
5. **Give specific feedback, then retry only the missing skill.** Use the table below. Ask the learner what was confusing and what they would do next time. Trainer review remains pending until an actual learner performs the task; leave elapsed time, help and retakes blank unless observed with consent. A request for help is instructional information, not a failure score.

| Observable outcome | Feedback / retry criterion |
| --- | --- |
| Saved wording matches the source and retains its date/limits | If it overclaims, point to the unsupported phrase and have the learner revise only that phrase, save and reopen. Clinical previews may be inspected as quarantined research; do not use them to practice treatment advice or bypass separate clinical confirmation. |
| Reload restores the chosen story/format/page and saved text | If local text is dirty, save or explicitly discard. For a revision conflict, retain working text, reload comparison and deliberately adopt/keep it. The retry succeeds when the learner can identify both versions and reopen their intended saved result. |
| Native edit reopens the same file; Studio preview distinction is understood | If a Studio export lacks the native edit, return to the chosen editor's own export. Do not repeat generation to try to synchronize separate artifacts. |
| Unknown save has one retained intent | Use **Retry same save** where offered. A known validation failure needs a corrected field, not a blind repeat; a quota failure stays preserved. |
| A missing font, image, sound or permission is explained | Stop that optional operation and retain the original. Choose an already declared safe fixture; never fabricate consent, silently substitute a font or call a public URL cleared media. |

### Optional planning exercise: turn evidence into a brief

In the separate authorized strategy organization, open **Add references**, preview a small owned JSON import, then deliberately add it. Select the person/resource and open **Evidence and constraints** and **Reference details**. Ask the learner to distinguish observed zero from unknown, dated availability from current availability, and a manager-reported permission from publication approval.

Add a target note. Under **Labels and evidence**, choose an existing source and a short label such as `needs-check`. Change selection before saving and check that the working note still names its original target. Save a named view and shortlist, build a report, explicitly choose its saved graph view and save. A pending view choice must be refreshed or cleared before handoff; saving report wording alone preserves its existing snapshot. **Confirm planning details** records only what the operator can honestly report for a proposed use, with the ordinary local date/time controls and displayed browser zone. Missing information is a valid outcome. Do not mark a real resource confirmed merely to get an Eligible label.

Choose an authorized draft destination, objective and proposed deliverables, then **Create review-required draft → Open draft in Campaign Studio**. Inspect retained notes, view, resource constraints and return link. The retry criterion is a reopened brief with the intended evidence/context and no invented approval. The [controlled engineering receipt](defiant-evidence-constraints-acceptance.md) proves the product journey; it is not the learner's practice record.

### Optional sound and recovery sessions

For sound, expand **Upload rights** and declare permission for an original fixture sound. In native **Audio**, **Uploads** is the available path: 16-bit stereo PCM WAV,44.1/48kHz, at most30seconds/6MiB. Library and Record explain why they are unavailable; they are not broken empty libraries. Add the sound to the design, save/reload, then use the ordinary MP4 or WebM download controls with sound enabled. Ask the learner to distinguish timeline duration, encoded file duration and actual listening; a decoded nonzero waveform alone is not an acoustic hearing assessment. Do not obtain a song merely for the lesson or treat a synthetic fixture's declaration as consent for other recordings.

For founder recovery, download the Operations recovery ZIP and retain its digest, choose a new empty destination, inspect the preview and map owners deliberately. After restoration, historical artwork is evidence: review the source as the current owner, then **Check reviewed source → Create editable copy**. Save/reload the new copy and inspect fonts/images before exporting. If the retained artwork has overlapping text or a placeholder, recovery fidelity has preserved a layout defect; fix the new copy deliberately rather than calling the restored design publish-ready. Use a disposable separately prepared installation for this lesson, never replace a working brand. See [fresh application recovery](fresh-install-recovery-neo.md).
