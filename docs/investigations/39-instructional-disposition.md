Prerequisite graph: source/rights and upstream version/license → common contracts/authority → isolated native import/edit/export → tenant/callback/recovery controls → measured resource/cost envelope → guided learner practice → independent performance/transfer. All referenced prerequisites are identified here; implementation evidence is missing, so readiness **fails**. The later3-stage choice cannot silently replace A's adapter-only/parallel option.

Both objectives A07 and B09 are currently unassessed by their proposed script-count assessments: alignment coverage for native-editor competence is **0of2**. This is a count of matched evidence, not a subjective quality score. Guided steps exist as prose, but worked examples reference missing functions; learner prerequisites and actual practice feedback are untested. Cognitive-load judgment is qualitative: A combines tokens, scene graphs, sources, motion and licensing; B adds CRDT/auth/storage and operations. Split into staged exercises rather than assigning invented numeric load scores. Event1–4 framing/objectives/content remain trainer-owned; operator/source experts must approve clinical/high-stakes examples. No human learner outcome experiment has run.

Required correction before learner exposure: replace unsupported claims with reviewed sources or explicitly unreviewed research prompts; deliver one actual worked native roundtrip; teach token/tenant identity and artifact/publication distinctions; assess native edit+export+recovery, not schema-only success; then test both marketer and founder tasks. Preserve retake options and require the full three-page reel and third-tenant/webhook transfers as separate capstones.
