# Organization and brand isolation pathway

Status: proposed specification supporting the approved [capability map](architecture/capability-map.md). See [evidence audit](TENANCY-EVIDENCE.md). This document defines acceptance and boundaries; it does not authorize implementation, deployment or billing activation. The sequence is container-runtime → durable-storage → identity-tenant-access → operator-workflow → gcp-pilot → managed-billing-exit.

## Ownership and authority

An account is a person authenticated by an identity provider. An organization is the customer/accountability boundary and owns brands. A brand is a workspace with editorial identity, profile, content and permissions. A preset is initial configuration copied into a brand, not an account, membership or authorization boundary. `liquidity_desk` and `vedic_dharma` are two brands in one organization for the pilot.

Proposed pilot roles: organization owner manages organization membership, brands and recovery; organization admin manages brands and grants without owner transfer; brand editor reads/edits/generates/schedules within granted brands; brand viewer reads within granted brands. Export permissions, provider configuration and billing permissions are explicit grants rather than consequences of a UI tab. Founder holds owner access; marketer receives only necessary brand editor grants. Exact role matrix, export approval and who may invite others require spec approval. Organization membership is necessary but does not automatically grant every brand. Owner recovery authority must be explicit and audited.

Authenticate every protected request, resolve current organization membership and brand grant on the server, then construct immutable request context `{ actorId, organizationId, brandId, permissions, requestId }`. A browser path/selector may request a brand; it cannot establish authority. Never keep an active organization/brand in process-global state. Reject absent/invalid context and unknown or foreign IDs; never fall back to the first backlog story. Revoked membership must prevent subsequent requests and queued work according to a documented revocation policy.

Every business record carries organization and brand ownership: profiles, strategies, pillars, sources, stories, drafts/versions, calendar slots, assets, generation traces, human edits, exports and jobs. Organization-level membership, billing and provider policy records carry organization scope; identity records are account-level. All repository lookups and writes require scope and validate ownership, including updates by ID. Asset object keys, signed URL issuance, cache keys, job payloads, event IDs and telemetry/export queries include that scope. Do not encode isolation solely in a guessed filename prefix. Public health probes contain no tenant content. Cross-customer provider credentials stay server-side in a secret boundary.

## Content readiness

Keep Liquidity Desk's existing profile as a separately versioned preset. Proposed CWJ preset: brand label Chai With Jai, slug `vedic_dharma`, Mona Sans/Inter, colors `#0B101B`, `#E85D04`, `#219EBC`, `#E5E7EB`, editable CWJ monogram. Exact strategy, value groups, pillars and approved source material require owner review rather than fabricated filler. Store profile revision on every generation so later brand editing does not rewrite history.

Separate source claim status (`unreviewed`, `approved`, `rejected`) from generated copy and generation provenance. Quarantine handoff clinical claims until source links, evidence quality, applicability and editorial approval are recorded. Do not seed unverified clinical numbers as verifiedFacts. Do not adapt financial regex scores into claims of clinical validity. Both brands require source-backed content; synthetic mode remains opt-in and visible. Missing source facts must fail clearly rather than invent default valuations, margins or physiological effects.

## Proposed authorization acceptance matrix

For every studio and admin API in `server/index.js`, exercise unauthenticated, foreign organization, same organization/no brand grant, brand viewer, brand editor and authorized administrative requests. Cover bootstrap, profile update, backlog creation, scheduling, generation, inline edits, assets, admin overview/config, batch, feedback export and graph access. Graph metadata may be public only after deliberate review; it must not expose secrets or customer records.

Use two organizations and at least two brands per organization. Try foreign IDs in body/path/query, mismatched org/brand pairs, guessed asset URLs, cache collisions, export cursors and background-job replays. A denial must produce no write, telemetry disclosure, provider call or billable usage. Authorized same-brand collaboration succeeds, while concurrent requests switching brands cannot contaminate each other. Browser tests verify tabs, reloads, deep links, role changes and revoked access. Job workers independently resolve trusted scope and permissions rather than trusting a submitted actor ID. Platform operators use separately audited privileges, not a universal admin UI toggle.

## Migration, recovery and customer exit

Before persistence migration, export any recoverable live in-memory state through an authorized maintenance mechanism and validate record counts; restarting first destroys that state. Do not claim that mechanism exists today. Import the single-profile data only into a designated organization and Liquidity Desk brand after owner confirmation. Never assign unknown provenance to CWJ automatically. Give old records stable IDs and explicit legacy/unreviewed provenance; preserve draft/edit lineage. Migration must be versioned, rerunnable without duplication, and reject mixed-scope or orphaned records. Back up before writes and rehearse restore in an isolated instance.

Use compatibility checks so older application versions cannot write a newer schema silently. Rollback restores a matching application, schema and data snapshot, with a declared cutover/recovery point; reverting code alone is insufficient once storage changes. Drain jobs before cutover and reconcile completed job IDs to avoid duplicate generations/usage events. If rollback cannot be lossless after new writes, stop writes and use a reviewed forward repair. Verify counts, ownership, assets and sample lineage after restore. Recovery objectives and backup retention remain owner decisions.

Ejection produces a versioned manifest containing only the authorized organization's/selected brands' data, assets and editable configuration, schema versions, checksums, source/provenance and lineage. It excludes other customers, service secrets, payment tokens and managed credentials. Provide a portable local container, validated import, local identity bootstrap or documented replacement identity adapter, and replaceable storage/provider settings. A successful exit is demonstrated by importing into a clean environment without managed-service credentials and performing authorized read/edit/export. Transfer custom-domain setup separately where applicable. Deletion after handoff requires separate owner confirmation, retention/legal decisions and a verified backup policy; exporting alone does not delete.

## Identity, billing and offer boundaries

Clerk is the preferred future identity/billing direction, not an approved implementation dependency. Verify official current capabilities when specifying integration. Keep stable internal account/org IDs and map external provider IDs; brand grants remain application authorization. Verify session validation, invites, organization membership, revocation, webhook signature/replay handling and self-hosted exit before choosing adapter details. No claim here establishes that Clerk covers the required payment methods or usage metering.

A usage event needs immutable event ID, organization/brand, operation, generation/job linkage, units, timestamp and outcome. Retry/replay must not double-charge. Distinguish provider cost accounting, billable consumption, entitlements and invoices. Define failed/synthetic/cancelled operation treatment before collecting billable usage. Billing/webhook failures must not silently delete customer work. Provider, payment and identity dependencies must be replaceable for self-hosted ejection.

Proposed offers remain $500/month plus $1,500 setup or $6,000 upfront. Resolve upfront duration, included usage/support/hosting, overages, refund/cancellation, data retention and exit terms. No open-source or proprietary license is selected by this spec. Confirm ownership and third-party licenses before distribution; public repository visibility alone does not grant a commercial redistribution license.
